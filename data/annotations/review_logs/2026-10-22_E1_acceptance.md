## Scope

This record covers the 22 tasks assigned prospectively to Expansion Calibration Batch E1 in `EXPANSION_BATCHES.json`.

E1 contains 98 RIUs:
- 43 critical
- 55 supporting
- 91 binary
- 7 ordinal

The batch spans all five study domains and all three difficulty levels. All three frozen prompt variants were considered for each task.

## Review disposition

The E1 annotation set and its methodological audit were checked and accepted by the study lead on 2026-10-22.

The following were explicitly inspected in the E1 draft:
- RIU coverage and unit boundaries
- criticality assignments
- binary versus ordinal scoring decisions
- all seven 0.5 anchors
- silence and negative-constraint handling
- CHECK-01 orthogonality
- EN/BN/BN-CS semantic alignment
- implicit safeguards
- diagnostic generality and avoidance of tool-specific answer prescriptions

No frozen prompt change, pilot-gold change, calibration change or protocol amendment was required.

## Decision

**E1 ACCEPTED.**

The 22 E1 records may be treated as completed expansion-calibration annotations for subsequent study assembly and validation. Final production-gold assembly remains a separate later release step.
