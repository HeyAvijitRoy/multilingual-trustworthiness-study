## Held-out design and sample-size rationale

Proposed target: **90 new validation-only fixture families**, outside the exposed 30 calibration fixtures and seven adjudication cases, with six unique families in each of 15 domain/language strata (five domains × EN/BN/BN-CS). In each stratum, construct two clearly acceptable, two clearly unacceptable and two borderline examples. Borderline means a difficult application with an accepted, defensible reference; a genuinely unresolved reference cannot be used as a decisive held-out label. Distinct families prevent language variants of one scenario being counted as independent cases. Reused templates or related scenarios must be identified as clusters.

Use new validation-only scenarios rather than changing frozen study prompts or annotating study-model outputs after inspecting their scores. Include negative constraints, conditional safeguards, contradictions, partial fulfillment, omissions, nonanswers and the operational/materiality boundaries diagnosed in adjudication. These diagnoses define coverage, not copied validation items or predetermined labels. Include both binary and explicitly anchored ordinal RIUs, at least 300 RIU judgments and 150 critical judgments, and exactly one designated safety-related task check per fixture, giving at least 90 primary safety checks. Any additional applicable checks are frozen and counted too. Final exact denominators and coverage are frozen before any candidate evaluation; extra checks must not be chosen after viewing disagreements. Units within a fixture are dependent and do not increase the independent fixture count.

Ninety cases is a resource-conscious challenge-set proposal, not an automatic sufficient sample. For illustration, with independent exchangeable checks, observing zero disagreements gives a one-sided 95% binomial lower agreement bound of 0.05^(1/n): approximately 90.50% for n=30, 95.13% for n=60 and 96.73% for n=90. Even 90/90 does not prove perfect reliability, and six fixtures per stratum cannot tightly characterize a domain/language interaction. Dependence reduces effective information; 90 checks clustered into only 30 families should not be advertised as 90 independent observations. The designed challenge distribution is not a random sample of production responses, so these bounds illustrate precision tradeoffs rather than establish population reliability.

If independent reference construction or resources cannot support 90 families, document the shortfall prospectively, preserve the existing thresholds, and report incomplete readiness. Do not silently reduce sample size after seeing results or claim 30 is inherently enough. A larger external study may be needed for general reliability claims even after a readiness gate passes.
