# Prompt corpus changelog

## v0.3: language-condition repair (2026-10-18) — FROZEN

**Scope:** `prompt_bn` and `prompt_bn_cs` were rewritten by hand for all 500 tasks.
- `prompt_en` is **byte-identical to v0.2**; no English prompt was changed.
- Task IDs, domains, subdomains and difficulty labels are unchanged.
- No model responses existed at the time of the change, so no results are affected.

**Authorization:** the study lead chose a versioned corpus repair, the route `data/annotations/PILOT_AUDIT.md` prescribes for confirmed language mismatches. v0.2 stays recoverable at commit `74604be07b2246e1d7f063088a35799721290e81`.

**Status: final and frozen.** The text was drafted with AI assistance (Claude) following `LANGUAGE_GUIDE.md`, then approved and frozen by the study lead on 2026-10-18 without a recorded independent human linguistic review (an AI pre-review and AI validation are logged in `data/annotations/review_logs/`). v0.3 is the frozen evaluation corpus pinned in `data/annotations/PROMPT_FREEZE.json`; any further change requires a new versioned release.

### Why

The v0.2 BN-CS prompts were generated by substituting lexicon words into the Bengali text. An AI pre-review (`data/annotations/review_logs/2026-10-18_AI_bengali_prereview.md`) found four problems with that approach:

1. **Grammatical corruption in BN-CS.** Examples: `impact-িত`, `evidence-িত`, `প্রো file`, about 30 wrong genitives such as `user-র`, and the Devanagari letter ট inside a Bengali word (SYN021).
2. **Meaning changes in BN-CS.** সিদ্ধান্ত ("conclusion") was replaced by `decision` in 8 tasks whose whole point is which conclusion is supported.
3. **Opaque or misleading BN calques**:

   | Concept | v0.2 Bengali | What it actually says |
   |---|---|---|
   | service account | সেবা অ্যাকাউন্ট | customer-service account |
   | critical production workflow | গুরুত্বপূর্ণ বাস্তব কাজ | "important real work"; "production" lost |
   | storage bucket | স্টোরেজ বালতি | a pail |
   | software corruption | সফটওয়্যার দুর্নীতি | bribery |
   | freezes | স্থির হয়ে যায় | "becomes still" |
   | supportability | সমর্থনযোগ্যতা | worthy of endorsement |
   | diagnostic | রোগনির্ণয় | medical diagnosis |
   | filter | ছাঁকনি | kitchen sieve |
   | CSV import | আমদানি | trade import |
   | appointment | সময় | "time" |
   | statistically significant | পরিসংখ্যানগতভাবে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ | "statistically important"; the distinction DEC070 tests is erased |

4. **Thin, English-light BN-CS.** The median English word share was 8%. Key domain terms often stayed as the same Bengali calque, so BN and BN-CS barely differed exactly where real code-switching happens.

There were also cue mismatches between languages:
- BN added "step by step" (PROC001).
- BN dropped superlatives such as "safest" and "longest".
- "Assumptions" and "estimates" were both rendered অনুমান (DEC100).
- "Long-term retention" was narrowed to skill retention (SYN100).

### What changed

**BN** is now natural contemporary Bengali.
- Everyday technical English words are written in Bengali script where that is what speakers say (ওয়ার্নিং, এরর, হেল্‌থ চেক, ক্লোন, সার্ভিস অ্যাকাউন্ট, রিটেনশন).
- Native vocabulary is used where it is natural (ব্যবহারকারী, অগ্রাধিকার, সময়সীমা).
- No Latin script except single-letter labels copied from EN (Group A, Model X). This fell from 7 rows in v0.2 to 0.

**BN-CS** is Bengali-matrix professional code-switching written in parallel with BN, sentence by sentence.
- Domain terms and professional verbs switch to English (`check করুন`, `service account`, `production workflow`).
- Generic vocabulary, postpositions and grammar stay Bengali.
- Suffixes follow the stem's pronunciation (`user-এর`, `policy-র`, `data-তে`).

**Both Bengali conditions:**
- keep every EN number, constraint, quantifier, superlative and output-format request;
- add no hints, examples or parenthetical glosses;
- keep the same polite register and Bengali digits.

**Ambiguity:** where EN is deliberately or incidentally ambiguous, the Bengali keeps the ambiguity rather than resolving it. For example, "long-term retention" became দীর্ঘমেয়াদি রিটেনশন in SYN004, SYN095 and SYN100.

| BN-CS English word share | v0.2 | v0.3 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Median | 0.083 | 0.375 |
| Interquartile range | 0.048–0.120 | 0.320–0.429 |
| Maximum | 0.270 | 0.531 |

### Resolution of the pilot-audit language flags

| Task | v0.2 flag | v0.3 |
|---|---|---|
| TECH006 | BN-CS `প্রো file`; BN "crash" weakened | `user profile`; BN ক্র্যাশ করছে |
| DEC006 | BN-CS `impact-িত` | `affect করছে`; "longest" restored in BN |
| CYBER009 | BN loses "production" and "service account" | সার্ভিস অ্যাকাউন্ট … প্রোডাকশনে চলা একটি গুরুত্বপূর্ণ ওয়ার্কফ্লো; BN-CS `service account`, `critical production workflow` |
| SYN100 | BN narrows retention; BN-CS conclusion → decision | রিটেনশন in both conditions; BN-CS `causal conclusion` |

These resolutions were accepted in the study lead's v0.3 approval and freeze.

### New automated guards (`scripts/validate_prompt_corpus.py`)

- **Numeric equivalence:** every number in EN must appear in BN and BN-CS (Bengali digits are normalized).
- **No added material:** no parenthetical glosses absent from EN.
- **BN script:** no Latin-script words in BN except single-letter labels.
- **Character set:** no non-Bengali Indic characters.
- **BN-CS morphology:** no vowel sign attached to a hyphen, and suffixes must match the English stem's final sound.
- **Matrix language:** BN-CS English word share at most 0.6.
- **Banned calques:** an extended list of deprecated calques, matched only at the start of a word.

The v0.2 corpus fails these checks; v0.3 passes them.

### Effect on annotations

- The 25 pilot RIU records keep their RIU text unchanged, because they are written against EN and EN is unchanged.
- Their `prompt_sha256` values were re-pinned to v0.3.
- Their status remains `method-audited`; language-equivalence review is still pending.
- `PROMPT_FREEZE.json` now records `corpus_version: 0.3` and the superseded v0.2 baseline.
- `.gitattributes` forces LF line endings under `data/` and `scripts/`, so Windows checkouts no longer alter the hashed bytes.
