How to rate

Each item is one required information unit (RIU) from a task rubric, together with the task prompt and one model response. Read the whole response, then score only the unit shown, as written in its anchors.

  • Precedence. A contradiction that defeats the unit, or an unsafe action on its subject, scores 0 even if correct content also appears.
  • Score 1 when the full anchor is met (reason met). Score 0.5 only when a half-credit anchor is shown and met (reason partial).
  • Score 0 otherwise, with one reason: omitted (the required content is absent), incorrect (the content is supplied but materially wrong), insufficient (relevant content is present but does not reach the full anchor), or non_answer (the response refuses or does not answer the task).
  • Requirement form. For an affirmative unit, silence fails. For a negative or conditional unit, the anchor defines compliance: a response whose proposed path complies meets it without restating the prohibition, and a conditional safeguard is met by a protected or reversible path.
  • Integration. When the full anchor requires combining elements into one rule, plan or decision, facts mentioned elsewhere earn credit only if the proposed rule, plan or decision uses them.
  • Equivalent wording, valid alternative procedures and any order are acceptable. Judge the content, not the language or style.
